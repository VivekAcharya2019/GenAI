NumPy (Numerical Python) is a Python library used for efficient numerical computing. It provides powerful data structures and mathematical operations for working with numbers, arrays, matrices, and multidimensional data.

Sentence Transformers converts text into vectors called embeddings that capture semantic meaning.
Allowing you to compare text based on meaning rather than just matching keywords.

Dot Product Similarity: Calculates the sum of the products of corresponding vector elements. Higher scores indicate stronger alignment, but scores are affected by vector magnitude.

Cosine Similarity: Measures the cosine of the angle between two vectors, focusing on their direction rather than magnitude. Higher values indicate more similar directions. Commonly used in semantic search and RAG.

Euclidean Distance (L2): Measures the straight-line distance between two vectors. Smaller distances indicate closer vectors.

Manhattan Distance (L1): Calculates the sum of the absolute differences between corresponding vector elements. Smaller distances indicate closer vectors.
